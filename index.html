Research

I study transient phenomena in the image plane, through both targeted and blind searches. My work ties together finding a signal inside an enormous volume of data, working out what detections and non-detections imply about the underlying population, and interpreting the physics of what was found.

In practice that means searching radio and optical survey images for sources that appear and disappear, modeling gamma-ray burst afterglows to measure the energy in the jet and the density of the gas around the explosion, and building the software when it does not already exist.

Software

  • RaTS Radio Transient Simulations. Computes transient rates for radio surveys by Monte Carlo simulation, across a range of light curve shapes and observing setups.
  • GRITS GPU Radio Image Transient Search. Flux-calibrates images, differences them, detects transients and applies quality control, implemented on the GPU and optimized for high data throughput.
  • Bifrost Contributor. High-throughput GPU streaming framework for radio telescopes.

Projects

  • TESS-Fermi results Searching TESS full-frame images for afterglows from poorly localized Fermi gamma-ray bursts.
